# ER/PR Reporting Guidelines

**Version:** ASCO/CAP 2020 Update
**Application:** Breast Carcinoma
**Last Updated:** January 2026

---

## Testing Indications

**Required for:**
- All newly diagnosed invasive breast carcinomas
- Recurrent breast cancer (if not previously tested)
- Metastatic disease (consider re-testing if original test >5 years old)

**May be deferred for:**
- Pure DCIS (institutional discretion)

---

## Interpretation Thresholds (ASCO/CAP 2020)

### Estrogen Receptor (ER)

**Positive:**
- ≥1% of invasive tumor cell nuclei show staining
- Any intensity of staining

**Negative:**
- <1% of invasive tumor cell nuclei show staining

### Progesterone Receptor (PR)

**Positive:**
- ≥1% of invasive tumor cell nuclei show staining
- Any intensity of staining

**Negative:**
- <1% of invasive tumor cell nuclei show staining

**NOTE:** Threshold lowered from 10% to 1% in 2010 guideline update to identify patients who may benefit from endocrine therapy

---

## Quantification Methods

### H-Score (Histoscore) Method **[Recommended]**

**Calculation:**
```
H-Score = (% weak staining × 1) + (% moderate staining × 2) + (% strong staining × 3)
Range: 0-300
```

**Example:**
- 20% weak (1+), 50% moderate (2+), 30% strong (3+)
- H-Score = (20×1) + (50×2) + (30×3) = 20 + 100 + 90 = 210

### Allred Score Method **[Alternative]**

**Proportion Score (PS):** 0-5
- 0 = no cells
- 1 = <1%
- 2 = 1-10%
- 3 = 11-33%
- 4 = 34-66%
- 5 = 67-100%

**Intensity Score (IS):** 0-3
- 0 = none
- 1 = weak
- 2 = moderate
- 3 = strong

**Total Allred Score = PS + IS (range 0-8)**

**Interpretation:**
- Allred 0-2: Negative
- Allred 3-8: Positive

**NOTE:** Allred score less commonly used; H-score or simple percentage preferred

## Clinical Interpretation

### Hormone Receptor Subtypes

| ER | PR | Clinical Significance |
|----|----|----------------------|
| Positive | Positive | Best prognosis, excellent endocrine therapy response |
| Positive | Negative | Moderate endocrine therapy response, consider HER2 status |
| Negative | Positive | Rare (<1%), verify accuracy, may respond to endocrine therapy |
| Negative | Negative | Poor prognosis if HER2-negative (triple-negative), consider chemotherapy |

### Low-Positive ER (1-10%)

**Special Considerations:**
- Borderline cases with uncertain endocrine therapy benefit
- ASCO/CAP recommends reporting as positive but noting low percentage
- Discuss with medical oncology for treatment decisions
- Consider repeat testing if clinically discordant

**Reporting:** "ER positive in X% of tumor nuclei. Note: Low-level ER expression (1-10%) may have limited predictive value for endocrine therapy benefit."

## Quality Control

### Internal Controls

**Positive Internal Control:**
- Normal breast ducts and lobules: Should show ER/PR nuclear staining
- If negative, test invalid

**Negative Internal Control:**
- Myoepithelial cells: Should be ER/PR negative
- Stromal cells: Variable, not reliable

### Pre-Analytical Requirements

**Fixation:**
- 10% neutral buffered formalin
- Fixation time: 6-72 hours
- Time to fixation: <1 hour from excision (ideally)

**Avoid:**
- Decalcification with strong acids (may cause false-negative results)
- Prolonged fixation (>72 hours) - may reduce antigenicity

## Special Scenarios

### Heterogeneous ER/PR Expression

**Definition:** Variability in staining intensity or distribution

**Reporting Approach:**
- Report overall percentage of positive nuclei
- Note heterogeneity in comment if clinically significant
- Example: "ER positive in 60% of tumor nuclei with heterogeneous distribution (focal areas with 90% positivity, other areas with 20%)."

### ER-/PR+ Pattern (Rare)

**Frequency:** <1% of breast cancers

**Action:**
- Verify staining quality (check internal controls)
- Consider repeat testing
- Review for sampling issues (e.g., different tumor area)
- If confirmed, report as found and note rarity

**Comment:** "Unusual ER-negative/PR-positive pattern. Recommend clinical correlation. Re-testing on alternative block may be considered."

### Post-Neoadjuvant ER/PR Changes

**Considerations:**
- ER/PR may change after neoadjuvant chemotherapy in ~10-20% of cases
- Usually conversion from positive to negative (worse prognosis)
- Recommend re-testing on residual tumor if present

**Reporting:** Note "post-neoadjuvant" status; compare to pre-treatment if available

---

## Common Pitfalls

**Pre-Analytical:**
- Under-fixation: False-negative results
- Delayed fixation: Variable results
- Thick sections: Difficulty assessing nuclear staining

**Analytical:**
- Scoring cytoplasmic staining: Only nuclear staining counts
- Scoring DCIS instead of invasive carcinoma: Ensure invasive component evaluated
- Scoring stromal/inflammatory cells: Count only tumor cells

**Reporting:**
- Failure to specify percentage: ASCO/CAP requires percentage, not just positive/negative
- Not commenting on low-positive (1-10%) cases: Uncertain benefit from endocrine therapy
